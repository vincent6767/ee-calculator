What is the CRS score?

The Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) is the points-based score Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) uses to rank candidates in the Express Entry pool for permanent residence. Your score is built from core human capital factors (age, education, language ability, work experience), points for an accompanying spouse or partner, skill transferability, and additional points such as a provincial nomination or sibling in Canada. Candidates with higher CRS scores are more likely to receive an invitation to apply.

What this tool does differently

The official IRCC calculator is built for a single pass: fill it in, get a score, and start over if you want to test a change. This planner adds the "what if" layer — retake a language test, finish a diploma, get a provincial nomination — and see exactly how each change moves your score, without retyping everything you already entered.

How scenario saving works

Save a scenario and it's stored in your browser under a name you choose, with the score and every input intact. Come back later, on the same browser and device, and it's waiting for you to reopen, edit, or compare — no account, no re-entry.

Your privacy

Your data never leaves your browser. There's no account, no server-side storage, and no tracking of your scores, language results, or other inputs. Analytics, if enabled, only records anonymous usage counts like button clicks — never your answers.
