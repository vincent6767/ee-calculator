CELPIP-G vs. IELTS: How Do They Compare for CRS Points?

Last updated August 20, 2026

Short answer

Neither test earns CRS points directly — both convert to the Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) scale, and identical CLB levels earn identical points in this calculator. A CELPIP-G score of 9 across all four abilities and an IELTS score of Speaking 7.0 / Listening 8.0 / Reading 7.0–7.5 / Writing 7.0 both convert to CLB 9 and score exactly the same. The only real question is which test you're more likely to score higher on.

Why your weakest skill matters more than your average

Language points also feed skill transferability, which gates on the minimum of your four abilities, not the average. Reaching CLB 9 in three skills and CLB 6 in the fourth caps transferability at the CLB 6 tier — on either test. See the Canadian work experience guide for how that combines with work experience.

Frequently asked questions

Do CELPIP-G and IELTS give different CRS points for the same CLB level?

No. This calculator scores by CLB level, not by test name, so a CELPIP-G score and an IELTS score that convert to the same CLB earn identical points.

Which test is easier for a higher CRS score?

Neither test is inherently worth more points — pick whichever test format you're more comfortable with and more likely to score a higher CLB on, since the CLB conversion is what determines your points.

Does the second-language test need to be CELPIP-G or IELTS too?

No — the second official language slot in this calculator accepts CELPIP-G, IELTS, or PTE Core, and its points are capped separately from the first-language score (up to 24 without a spouse, 22 with one).
